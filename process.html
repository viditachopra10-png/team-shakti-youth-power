HOW IT WORKS

The Process

The process connects carbon capture with the production of sustainable methanol.

01

Collect

Exhaust gases from industrial sources are collected for carbon capture.

02

Capture

An amine solution is used to trap carbon dioxide from the collected gases.

03

Release

The solution is heated so that the captured carbon dioxide can be released as a concentrated gas.

04

Transform

The captured CO₂ is used as a raw material in the sustainable methanol production pathway.

05

Reuse

The capture solution can be reused in the carbon capture cycle.

THE RESULT

Carbon becomes part of the solution.

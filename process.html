THE PROCESS

How It Works

From industrial exhaust to captured CO₂ and eventually toward sustainable methanol.

01

Collect Exhaust Gases

Exhaust gases from industrial sources are collected for carbon capture.

02

Prepare the Amine Solution

An amine solution is prepared to capture carbon dioxide from the exhaust gases.

03

Capture CO₂

The exhaust gas is passed through the solution, allowing the amine to capture CO₂.

04

Release the CO₂

The CO₂-rich solution is heated so that the captured carbon dioxide can be released.

05

Store the Captured CO₂

The released carbon dioxide is collected and stored for further processing.

06

Move Towards Methanol Production

The captured carbon becomes a feedstock for the sustainable methanol pathway explored by our project.

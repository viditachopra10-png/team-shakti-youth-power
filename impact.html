Our Impact

A project focused on reducing waste, rethinking carbon and inspiring action.

Reduce

Our concept explores ways of reducing the environmental impact of industrial carbon emissions.

Reuse

Instead of treating captured CO₂ only as waste, we explore its potential as a useful resource.

Inspire

Our project encourages young people to think about how science and technology can solve environmental challenges.

Educate

Through our website, carbon calculator and challenges, we aim to make sustainability easier for students to understand and practise.

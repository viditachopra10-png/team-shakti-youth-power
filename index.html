01 — THE PROBLEM

Industrial CO₂ is a growing challenge.

01

Carbon Emissions

Industrial activities release large amounts of carbon dioxide into the atmosphere, contributing to climate change.

02

Fossil Fuel Dependence

Many industries and transport systems still depend heavily on fossil fuels.

03

Need for Cleaner Solutions

We need innovative technologies that can reduce emissions while producing useful resources.

02 — OUR SOLUTION

From CO₂ to sustainable methanol.

Our project explores a pathway where captured industrial CO₂ and seawater can be used to produce sustainable methanol — a useful chemical and fuel.

03 — HOW IT WORKS

Our process, step by step.

01

Collect Industrial Exhaust

Exhaust gases from industrial sources are collected for carbon dioxide capture.

02

Capture CO₂

An amine solution is used to capture CO₂ from the exhaust gases.

03

Release & Store CO₂

The captured CO₂ is released using controlled heating and stored for further processing.

04

Produce Sustainable Methanol

The captured carbon is used as a feedstock in the production of sustainable methanol.

04 — IMPACT

Why does our idea matter?

Reuse

Instead of treating CO₂ only as waste, our concept explores using it as a valuable resource.

Renew

Sustainable methanol could provide an alternative pathway for cleaner fuels and chemical production.

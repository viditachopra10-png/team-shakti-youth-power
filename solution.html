Our Solution

Our idea is to turn captured industrial CO₂ into something useful instead of allowing it to remain only an emission.

THE IDEA

Sustainable Methanol from Industrial CO₂ and Seawater

Team Shakti explores a pathway where industrial carbon dioxide can be captured and used as a raw material for sustainable methanol production.

By connecting carbon capture with fuel production, the project aims to explore how an environmental challenge could become part of a useful solution.

CO₂ RESOURCE

Capture

Industrial carbon dioxide is captured from exhaust gases.

Transform

The captured carbon becomes a raw material for the process.

Produce

The goal is sustainable methanol production.
